# COMBAT SYSTEM

## Combat Flow

Target Selection
→ Move Into Range
→ Attack
→ Hit Check
→ Damage Calculation
→ Loot

## Hit Chance

HitChance = Accuracy / (Accuracy + Evasion)

Minimum 5%
Maximum 95%

## Damage Formula

Damage = ((Attack * SkillMultiplier) - Defense)

Minimum Damage = 1

## Critical Strike

Critical Damage = Final Damage × 2

## Damage Types

- Physical
- Fire
- Cold
- Lightning
- Poison

## Resistances

Range: 0% ~ 75%

FinalDamage = Damage × (1 - Resistance)

## Status Effects

- Burn
- Poison
- Slow
- Stun

## Death

Monster death triggers:
- Experience
- Gold
- Loot Roll

Player death triggers:
- Respawn in Town
