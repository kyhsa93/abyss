# Direction

What the playtest job currently believes is wrong with this game, and what would
show it wrong. `docs/playtest.md` says how this file is kept; the short version
is that every session either confirms a line, sharpens it, or drops it, and
seven standing lines is the cap.

Seeded on 2026-09-24 by the round that built the job. Folded on 2026-10-03 to
the 700-line cap (`docs/playtest.md`'s rule); the full history behind every
line below lives in `sessions.jsonl` and git, not here.

## Standing

### 1. Doing nothing wins, so nothing you do is a decision

A body that presses nothing (`idle`, and the zero-ability styles `dodge`/`flee`/`wander`/`mash`'s blind round-robin) gets carried to a clean, often-healthier win by the other 9-24 AI raiders, across nearly every role, boss and difficulty tried, so the one body a player steers is not load-bearing — the AI carrying it is the mode working as designed, the finding is only that the player's own presses show no visible consequence.

**Disproved by** any cell where a zero-/blind-action style loses outright while `good` wins, same boss/size/difficulty — found for real three times (Bloodgorged's `fester`, Bloodgorged's `gorge` against forced-melee steering, The Confluence's `infection` for non-tank roles) and narrowed by role (tank-exclusion-by-design on Confluence/Crimson Gift/several hound bosses). Several apparent "enrage beats a played pull" losses (Last Whisper, Three Crowns, Skyward Deck) turned out to be an engine quirk, not a raid loss: `src/sim/sim.ts:778-792` ends the pull the instant the single `isPlayer` actor dies, before the real wipe check, regardless of the other 24 — so those are the driven body dying to its own exposure, not the AI failing.

### 2. The walk in and the fight are the same screen, and the player cannot tell

`screen()` reads `fight` during a corridor walk, a boss fight, and a wipe alike — a driver watching only `screen` can stand in a room for fifteen minutes believing a fight it never started is still running.

**Disproved by** a session that reaches a boss by walking, from a fresh save, and can say at every moment which of the two it was in from the picture alone.

- 2026-09-24: Walked threshold → vigil → spire on a fresh touch save, shooting every chamber. `screen()` stayed `fight` throughout as expected, but the boss name/health bar across the top only draws once `mode()` is `raid` — a player who knows to look for that bar can tell travel from a fight without reading `mode()`. Leaning disproved, but narrowed: untested whether a first-time player notices to look, since this session already knew what it was checking for.

### 3. A wipe on the way between rooms cannot be retried, and stalls the evening

`docs/playtest.md` and the README both say a wipe should cost the pull, not the night; the walk between rooms is explicitly a fight too (`mode` reads `travel` while it runs). Filed as #271.

**Root cause found and commented on #271, from source** (`src/main.ts`): `restart()` resets `run.carried` via `wipedRoom()`, but `walkTo()`'s first call, `harvest()`, only acts while `state.mode === 'travel'` and reads `carriedOut(state)` off the still-dead corridor state, clobbering the reset with every actor's share back to `-1`; `carryInto()` then paints the brand-new corridor dead before its first tick. Boss-room retries never hit this path (`state.mode` is `'raid'`), which is the whole reason PULL AGAIN works for a boss and never for a corridor.

**Disproved by** a travel-mode wipe, anywhere in the building, where PULL AGAIN does restart the room — effectively closed by the mechanism above; stands only until `docs/upkeep.md`'s gate picks the fix up.

- 2026-10-01: 17th confirmation — a `dodge`-style wipe (mage:frost, 25-heroic), the first time `dodge` itself lost to THE VIGIL's watchmen after five prior clean crossings; argues "dodge never stalls" was a spec gap in what had been tried, not a property of the style. Same retry failure.
- 2026-09-29: Mechanism read from source (above), closing the "is it `cross()`-specific or pad-ride-specific" question the 9th and 13th confirmations had left open: it is `harvest()` running once too many times, not pads. This sub-line is done — nothing further to add by playing more cells.
- 2026-09-29: 13th confirmation — first reproduction *outside* THE VIGIL (a westclimb pack kill mid-pad-ride to `mooring`), overturning "at least in THE VIGIL" as a qualifier; the room `evening` calls stuck (`mooring`) is not the room the party actually died in (`westclimb`), because the stuck name follows the pad's destination, not the death's location.
- 2026-09-29: 12th confirmation — first clean `flee` VIGIL crossing (after two `flee` wipes), and a second westclimb stall with a very different `closestGot` (553 against the first stall's 12) — argues the watchmen catching the party is seed-luck, not class speed or style.
- 2026-09-28: 11th confirmation — tested whether `melee`'s one clean crossing (2026-09-25, rogue, the roster's fastest class) was a style or class-speed effect; a slow-tier class (warlock) under `melee` wiped instead, pointing at class speed doing the work in the one clean pass rather than the style itself.
- 2026-09-28: 10th confirmation — first wipe reached by bypassing `cross()` entirely (`mode=raid` plus the driver's own `walkto`), and it failed the retry identically, ruling out `cross()`'s own re-entry handling as the cause.
- 2026-09-25 through 2026-09-28: the identical retry failure (`hero.hp=0`, every ability slot `locked`, an instant 0-second re-wipe, `fault:evening-stuck`) reproduced across eleven further specs and five styles (`mash`, `wander`, `auto`, `flee`, `melee`), both sizes and both difficulties, always inside THE VIGIL until the 13th confirmation above. Filed as #271 on 2026-09-25 with journal lines and scripts; every later confirmation commented on the same issue rather than refiling.
- 2026-09-25: Opened — paladin:retribution, 10-normal, `evening wander`, wiped in THE VIGIL at 40.9s; PULL AGAIN left `outcome` stuck at `wipe` and the evening declared itself stuck. A same-profile reload did not recover it either — `src/main.ts`'s own resume path never ran, landing on a brand-new raid-setup screen instead.

### 4. A battleground's own setup is remembered less reliably than a raid's

Picking The Long Haul (escort) on the battleground setup screen and reloading loses the pick — the front page comes back on RAID, not BATTLEGROUND — while picking Conquest and reloading survives identically. `loadMode()` (`src/main.ts`) accepts only `raw === 'conquest' || raw === 'flags'` and falls back to raid on anything else, though `saveSetup()` writes whatever `mode.bg` actually is, escort included. Filed as #272.

**Disproved by** a fix landing and a reload after picking escort coming back on BATTLEGROUND/escort rather than RAID.

- 2026-10-01: Narrowed to a page reload specifically — in-app BACK navigation (setup screen → class pick → back, and class pick → home → back into RAID) preserves the pick correctly both times (`after-back-from-bgsetup.png`, `class-select-reentered.png`), because back-navigation re-reads the live in-memory `mode.bg` and never calls `loadMode()` at all. Confirms widening `loadMode()`'s accepted set to include `escort` is the whole fix, not half of one.
- 2026-09-24: Opened. Conquest played straight and survived a reload; The Long Haul (escort) picked, left without pulling, reloaded — pick gone, front page defaulted to RAID. Filed as #272 with both play-script segments and the before/after screenshots.

### 5. A `carried` save only carries within one `playbot` run, not between them

`docs/playtest.md` calls `carried` "how anything about a *second* evening gets seen at all" — but `scripts/playbot.ts` picks a new dev-server port every invocation (`5200 + ((pid + attempt*37) % 300)`), and `localStorage` is scoped per origin/port, so `--profile` reusing the same Chromium user-data dir does not reuse the same storage bucket across two separate `playbot` calls, only within one. Filed as #273.

**Disproved by** a fix (now known to need only a fixed port per `--profile`) landing in `playbot.ts`, and a `carried` session across two separate real `playbot` invocations actually reading back a prior one's state.

- 2026-10-02: Fix side tested, not just the failure. A one-off script ran the exact invocation lifecycle twice back-to-back on a fixed port (5900) against a scratch profile: phase 1 set class `warrior:arms` and name `PORTFIX1` then tore down; phase 2's fresh `vite`+context on the same port read both back on load, confirmed through the real UI too. Holding the port fixed is sufficient on its own — no snapshot/restore step needed — which narrows #273's fix to exactly that.
- 2026-09-25: Opened. A carried `mode=menus` session's RECORD screen read `0 pulls · 0 kills` despite the same profile having wiped The Two Flasks in a real prior session — `abyss.history` had been written once and never read back. Seven straight invocations against the same `--profile` printed seven different ports. Means every other `carried` cell in `sessions.jsonl` that relied on a separate prior session's state was actually running on a fresh origin in disguise.

### 6. THE VIGIL stalls any evening at one fixed point, win or lose

An `evening` walked into THE VIGIL often fails to cross it: not a wipe, the
party stays alive at full health dealing and taking nothing, converged on the
same few square units regardless of style, spec or seed. Filed as #281.
Source reads since have surfaced two concrete mechanisms behind the shape:
`roomUnderfoot()` keys off `partyMiddle()`'s lagging centroid rather than the
player, so a corridor fight reads as room-unchanged or even room-changed-
backward; and `cross()`'s own door-pick can silently self-loop, landing
unrelated runs on the same computed coordinate to 13 decimal digits. The
surviving theory is seed-rolled watchman/doorway placement, not style, class
or size/difficulty, decides clean vs. stall.

**Disproved by** an evening crossing THE VIGIL under any style from a fresh
save at the stalled size/difficulty -- met once on the letter of it
(`good`, 2026-09-27, the exact opening cell) but read as narrowing toward
seed-luck rather than a clean disproof, since a dozen+ clean/stall pairs
since share every other axis.

- 2026-10-03: priest:discipline (`moveSpeed=158`) `wander` wiped on the same cell druid:guardian opened on; 3 wipes + 1 stall, zero clean crossings under `wander` at any class speed tried (158/167/167) (journal: `fault:evening-stuck {"at":"vigil","after":"wipe","rooms":3}`).
- 2026-10-03: `melee`'s class-speed theory narrowed to a threshold -- shaman:elemental (`moveSpeed=167`) crossed clean same as rogue (178), while warlock/priest (158) both wiped; the split sits between 158 and 167, not "fastest class only" (journal: `closestGot=8, presses=3`).
- 2026-10-02: the spire/westclimb/oratory/eastclimb door-pick-vs-arrival mismatch (see below) confirmed on a 4th spec, first time at 25-heroic, settling that size/difficulty does not predict it (journal: `"from":"spire","to":"spire","closestGot":1889`).
- 2026-10-02: same mismatch hit two more specs (rogue:assassination, druid:balance) at the identical junction, same farthest-unvisited door pick each time before self-looping.
- 2026-10-01: a westclimb self-loop stall's coordinates landed within 1 unit of `x` and bit-identical `y` (13 digits) of two prior unrelated runs (different class/size/day) -- `cross()` computing the same steering target regardless of who walks it (journal: `"hero":{"x":-1383.33...,"y":-6610.624094474852}`, `closestGot":551-553`).
- 2026-10-01: one same-spec/style/size `flee` pair produced both a VIGIL wipe and a clean crossing with no axis differing but the wall clock -- decided by `Date.now()`-keyed `roomSeed`, not by `flee` itself.
- 2026-09-29: a same-class, same-tier split undercut both the speed and style theories at once -- druid:balance/`flee` crossed clean while druid:guardian/`wander` stalled, identical `moveSpeed` (167), same 10-normal tier.
- 2026-09-28/29: `dodge` reached 3-for-3 clean crossings (10-normal and 25-heroic both), the one style that has never once stalled THE VIGIL.
- 2026-09-27: mechanism read straight from source -- `roomUnderfoot()` tests `partyMiddle()`'s centroid, not the player; a lagging huddle during a corridor fight reads as room-unchanged or room-changed-backward (`wander` stalled SPIRE/WEST CLIMB this way) (journal cite: `src/main.ts:994`, `partyMiddle` comment on trailing by its own width).
- 2026-09-27: first clean crossing matching the hypothesis's own disprove condition on the letter of it -- `good`, same class/style/size/difficulty/cell as the 2026-09-25 opening stall, only the seed differing; read as narrowing toward seed-luck, not a full disproof.
- 2026-09-26: `good`, then `dodge`/`flee`/`mash`, crossed clean specifically at 25-heroic after stalling at 10-normal, building a "0 stalls at 25-heroic vs. 4 at 10-normal" tally -- later undercut by the 2026-09-29 same-tier split above.
- 2026-09-26: THE WEST CLIMB (not VIGIL) also burned a full room budget parked at a door, same shape as VIGIL's stalls -- the mechanism is not specific to this one corridor.
- 2026-09-25: corrected -- `melee`'s one clean crossing (rogue:assassination) did not replicate on slower classes (warlock, priest both wiped); the one clean pass reads as class `moveSpeed` (178) doing the work, not the style.

### 7. A battleground does not carry a passive body the way a raid does

A style that presses nothing (`flee`/`dodge`/`idle`) costs its own team the match, the mirror of [[#1]]'s raid-idle finding: the AI party carries an idle raid member but does not carry an idle battleground teammate.

**Disproved by** a battleground where a style that presses nothing wins, at any map — not yet disproved; the closest counter-case (2026-09-27, below) complicates rather than overturns it.

- 2026-10-03: idle tank (paladin:protection, conquest, fresh) survived exactly like every other idle run (`hits=0 hitsPerMin=0 taken=0 takenPerMin=0 died=false byMechanic={}`), but all four AI teammates died (vs 3/4 for the matching priest-idle cell) and the match ended faster and further behind (fightTime=132, 126-400 vs the priest's 155, 199-400) — first evidence that *which* slot sits idle matters, since the roster carries only one tank; open question: a `good`-style tank pull on the same cell, to see if a played tank actually anchors the point any better than an idle one.
- 2026-10-01: first true `idle` match played to its own conclusion (priest:shadow, conquest, fresh) — `hp=1350/1350` untouched the whole 155s, `inDanger=0%` both reads (`87s bill hits=0 hitsPerMin=0 taken=0 takenPerMin=0 inDanger=0% aliveParty=4/5`), while 3 of 4 AI teammates died and the match ended in defeat 199-400; screenshots show "You" simply never found, standing far from where the fighting is rather than getting caught.
- 2026-09-28: `good`-dies-almost-immediately extends to a healer — shaman:restoration under `good` on flags died at 14.5s with `hits=0 hitsPerMin=0 taken=1523 takenPerMin=5900-6300 died=true byMechanic={}` the whole pull; the party spawns clustered on top of the enemy "boss" actor's position and `good`'s beeline-at-`boss` steering walks the body straight into it. - 2026-09-28: closes the melee-capable-kit question, and corrects a standing misreading. ### Not yet filed

- **Three Crowns' untouchable-decoy steering gap.** Every toward-boss driver style (`melee`, `good`) walks onto `hud().boss`'s raw coordinate, which is sometimes the untouchable decoy, and spends the whole fight at 100% `thirst` — `hud()` exposes only one boss body's position, so no driver style can ever learn which of the three is real. A real player gets an unambiguous visual (`drawCourt`'s filled disc/warm ring) the vocabulary cannot see. `dodge`/`wander` both answered this differently (see [[#1]]): `dodge` never engages the puzzle at all (no toward-boss term), `wander` can still win outright. Driver gap, not a game bug — worth an enhancement note on `hud()` exposing which body is real, once the gate reopens.
- **Enrage/wipe outcome conflated with a real raid loss.** [[#1]]'s own `sim.ts:778-792` finding (the pull ends the instant the single `isPlayer` actor dies, before the real wipe check) means every "boss enraged and the raid lost" reading in this file's own history before 2026-09-29 needs to be re-read as "the driven body died," not "the other 24 failed." Nothing to file — it's a correction to how this file itself has been interpreting `outcome=enrage`/`wipe`, folded into [[#1]] above.
- **The banner/report overlap on kill screens.** Multiple earned banners (First Blood, Heroic, Full Raid, A Clean Board, time-based ones) stack directly over the damage board and the "died Ns" line, bleeding through on several different fights/sizes. Already open as #275/#283; reproduced again on 2026-09-25/26/27 heroic kills with no new mechanism — not re-filed, just noted so a future session doesn't re-discover it from scratch.
- **Stale encounter/affix banners.** A daily's own banner (boss name, affix) can read a stale value for one beat after a new instance loads, narrowed on 2026-10-02 to specifically the frame between `tap start` and the first `state` read — cosmetic, one or two frames, not yet worth a `bug` label on its own.
- **A corridor between two rooms is itself a fight worth testing on its own terms.** `docs/playtest.md`'s own observation, confirmed repeatedly: `mode` reads `travel`, packs can wipe a party, and winning it advances the evening with no further input. Already covered by [[#3]]/[[#6]] for the ways it breaks; this entry is the reminder that a clean corridor crossing is not automatically "nothing happened" and deserves its own look occasionally, not just as a means to reach a boss room.
- **`auto`-style's silent fallback to `good` on non-touch viewports.** `style=auto` relies on a touch-only autocast toggle; on a desktop/mouse viewport the control does not exist, so the driver logs `fault:no-such-control`/`no-autocast-toggle ... playing good instead` and silently substitutes `good`. Confirmed in three different modes (`walk`, `battleground`, `daily`) — a driver-only quirk, but worth remembering before reading an `auto` session's results as actually testing `auto` on desktop.
- **A real player's path through the front door is still mostly untested.** Nearly every session reaches a fight via an invite hash (`open #b=...`) rather than front page → mode pick → class pick → PULL, because the hash is faster to script. Only a couple of sessions have gone all the way through the real menu chain. Worth a session that deliberately plays the slow way end to end, since the hash shortcut could be hiding a menu-chain bug no session has looked for.
- **Hound-style ground-mechanic targeting needs a same-style controlled pair before trusting it.** [[#1]]'s own caution: two runs with different styles consume the shared RNG stream differently, so a mechanic-target gap between an idle and a good pull is not safe to read as positional unless a same-style (both idle, or both good) pair is run first. Still outstanding.
- **Convenience/tap-count findings are accumulating informally but not yet measured with `ui`.** Several sessions have commented on how many taps it takes to get from a wipe back into the same pull, or from the front page to a fight, but none has yet run `ui` specifically to turn that into the hard number `docs/playtest.md` asks for. Worth doing once a session is not otherwise occupied re-verifying #271/#281's backlog.
- **The real menu-driven RAID unlock path vs. the invite-hash shortcut can diverge in what it shows.** A couple of sessions noted the raid-setup screen defaults differently depending on whether a fresh save arrived via the real class-pick flow or an invite hash; not yet characterized well enough to be its own line.
- **A `wander`/`mash` healer still gets carried by AI healers even at zero self-output.** Seen clearly inside [[#1]] (The Three Crowns, paladin:holy, 0 heals cast all fight) but also surfaces in non-Crowns cells; kept under [[#1]] rather than split out, since it's the same "passive body costs nothing" shape, just measured through a healing-specific lens.
- **Label-matcher fallback and lowercase `key escape` are driver-only quirks**, not game findings — noted here once so a future session recognizes the symptom (`targets` partial-matching a label it shouldn't, or `key Escape` vs `key escape` mattering) rather than re-diagnosing it as a game bug.

## Tried and dropped

- **Battleground player-respawn stall** (raised 2026-09-25, dropped 2026-09-26): a chained-`play` run reading a dead body as stuck past `RESPAWN_LATE` was `playbot`'s own abort-on-`hero()===null` sampling gap, not a game bug — bare `state`-polling after the kill always revived cleanly in ~10s, confirmed on three separate maps/specs. Even near-bare sampling can land in the gap if a `play` call runs nearby; only a `play` call followed by pure `wait`/`state` settles it.
- **`tap refresh` on SETTINGS crashes `playbot`'s driver, not the game** (2026-10-01): `reloadFresh()` ends with a bare `location.reload()`, which kills the page's JS context before `tap()`'s own 80ms read-back lands — a driver assumption, not a bug. A one-off script driving the touch event directly and racing `page.waitForNavigation` confirmed the button itself works: a name written to `localStorage` survived the reload and read back correctly through the real SETTINGS field. Go straight to a one-off script for this button rather than scripting it into a `.play` file.
