# Paperdoll credits

The player himself is rendered from one kit, so that half is
one line.

* **Modular RPG Characters** — System G6 (Qoma), CC0 1.0
  <https://opengameart.org/content/modular-rpg-characters>

What he is holding is not in that kit — it has no weapon
at all — so the meshes come off another shelf, one a word.
Generated from the table `pipeline/arms.py` renders from,
so the list cannot fall behind the sheets.

* **axe** — `Axe-Double`, Quaternius, CC0 1.0
* **dagger** — `Dagger`, Quaternius, CC0 1.0
* **mace** — `Doublesided-Hammer`, Quaternius, CC0 1.0
* **staff** — `Spear`, Quaternius, CC0 1.0
* **sword** — `Sword`, Quaternius, CC0 1.0

Rendered by `pipeline/render_paperdoll.py` at this game's own
projection; the renders are art this repository made, and the
models and textures under them are their authors'.
